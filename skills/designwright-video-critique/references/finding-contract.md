# Video Finding Contract

```text
Finding: VID-<stable-id>
Severity: blocker | high | medium | low
Classification: objective defect | likely defect | opinion | uncertainty
Timestamp: <start>-<end>
Shot: <shot-id or unknown>
Segment: <audio/text segment if known>
Problem: <observable defect>
Why it matters: <viewer, factuality, realism, accessibility, or channel consequence>
Evidence: <artifact/model/check + reproducible location>
Proposed change: <smallest bounded repair>
Unchanged: <facts, timing, style, source, or other invariants>
Owner: producer | editor | voice | graphics | project owner
Status: open
```

Do not claim an uncertain model judgment as an objective defect.
