# Audiovisual Review Protocol

Use multiple passes rather than one vague "does this look good?" judgment.

1. Deterministic media integrity: duration, format, black/frozen frames, audio presence, clipping/loudness, long unintended silence, caption presence.
2. Transcript reconciliation: compare rendered speech to approved script and flag missing/duplicated/materially changed segments.
3. Audio-only listener: naturalness, pronunciation, cadence, voice identity, seams, pops, mix.
4. Visual-only reviewer: relevance, authenticity, generated artifacts, fake UI, warped text, repetition, caption readability, pacing.
5. Synchronized audiovisual reviewer: narration/visual alignment, transition timing, emotional fit, intentional-editing quality.
6. Factual/source alignment: claims and documentary-looking visuals map to evidence.
7. Format review: long-form or Short/Reel-specific requirements.

A local/open reviewer is preferred when benchmark-qualified. One model is never the sole realism authority for high-value or borderline cases.
