# Video Production Contract

A complete contract records:

- production ID;
- topic and audience;
- research/source revision;
- thesis and hook;
- target long-form runtime;
- Short/Reel target and independent hook/payoff;
- section list and purpose;
- factual claims and evidence IDs;
- voice profile;
- visual-source policy;
- capability requests;
- privacy class;
- external-cost ceiling;
- generated-media allowance/disclosure;
- QA requirements;
- publication boundary;
- approver and status.

## Capability request

For each required capability record:

```text
capability:
purpose:
quality_gate:
privacy_class:
input:
output:
preferred_cost: local/free
paid_escalation_condition:
fallback_behavior:
evidence_required:
```

Do not put a vendor name in the requirement unless the human explicitly approved a vendor-specific experiment.
